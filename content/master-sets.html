Master Sets

The term master set means different things depending on context. There are three common goals:

  • Master Set (by release) — every card from a specific set release, excluding promotional cards. This means the full numbered set plus variants like reverse holos and secret rares, but promos are out of scope.
  • Grandmaster Set — a full master set plus all promotional cards associated with that release. The most complete version of a set collection.
  • Master Set (by Pokémon) — every card ever printed featuring a specific Pokémon, across all sets and releases. A popular goal for collectors who focus on a favorite Pokémon rather than a specific set.

PokeMasterList

PokeMasterList is a great resource for collectors building a master set by Pokémon — it catalogs every card printed for a given Pokémon, making it easy to see what exists and what you're still missing.

Tracking your progress

Card-Codex supports all three types of master set collecting. It tracks set completion including variants and promos, so whether you're chasing a master set, a grandmaster set, or every card of a specific Pokémon, you can see your progress at a glance.
