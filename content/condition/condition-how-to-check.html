Quick self-check

  1. Use a clean, dry surface and good lighting.
  2. Check both sides, all four corners, and every edge.
  3. Tilt it under the light to reveal scratches, dents, and surface marks.
  4. Photograph any defects if you plan to sell.
  5. Use the marketplace's detailed standard to choose a condition, then sleeve the card.

Common issues

  • Whitening and scratches: Judge their size, number, and severity. A few slight edge marks do not automatically rule out Near Mint.
  • Creases and dents: Inspect both sides. TCGplayer caps a creased card at Moderately Played at best; more severe damage can require a lower condition.
  • Curving or warping: Record what you see and check the applicable standard. Don't press, heat, or dampen a card to improve its appearance.
  • Peeling, stains, or liquid exposure: Photograph the affected area and consult the detailed criteria rather than judging the rest of the card alone.

For borderline cases, use TCGplayer's illustrated defect definitions. If you're still uncertain, describe the flaws and ask an experienced card shop for a second opinion.
