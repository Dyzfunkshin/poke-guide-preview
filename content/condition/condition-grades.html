These are broad summaries of TCGplayer's condition categories. Use the rules of the marketplace where you're buying or selling.

  • Near Mint (NM): Minimal wear with a few slight imperfections allowed. It does not mean flawless.
  • Lightly Played (LP): Minor wear or scratches, without significant defects.
  • Moderately Played (MP): More noticeable wear; some limited bends or creases can qualify under the standard.
  • Heavily Played (HP): Substantial wear or more severe defects.
  • Damaged: Problems that meet the marketplace's damage criteria, including compromised card integrity.

These labels are not interchangeable with a grading company's numerical score. See grading for that separate process.
