    • Web
  • Features: online collection manager with price display, collection and wishlist management, and master set tracking capabilities.
  • What it tracks: Pokemon.
  • Summary: Card-Codex is a newer collection tracker focused on raw card values and master set completion. It lets you log individual cards, see the value of each card, track progress toward completing each set, and view totals for both individual sets and your entire collection. Because it's still early in development, there are occasional bugs and rough edges, but it's improving quickly and has strong potential to become a top option for set-focused collectors.
