    • Android
    • iOS
  • Features: instant card scanning, daily market data, collection organization by set, rarity, and condition, portfolio value tracking, wishlist tools, and price alerts.
  • Who it helps: collectors who want to log cards quickly from their phone instead of manually entering everything into a spreadsheet or desktop-first tracker.
  • Summary: Holodex is a mobile-first trading card collection app built around fast camera scanning and simple portfolio tracking. It is especially useful if you like checking prices, sorting cards by set or condition, and watching wishlist targets from one place.
  • Things to know: Holodex is focused on iOS and Android rather than a full web app, so it makes the most sense if you manage your collection on your phone. You will want an account if you want synced tracking and alerts across devices, and like other tracking apps, its pricing should be treated as a guide rather than a final buy/sell comp.
