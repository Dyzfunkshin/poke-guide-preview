How do I identify my card?

Start with the name, language, set, and collector number. Then match the artwork, foil finish, and any stamps. Cards that look similar can be different printings.

Find the matching card

Add terms to the Catalog Explorer search bar and the list will filter to matching cards. The more relevant terms you add, the narrower the results. A card name and collector number will usually get you very close and help you find variants to compare.

Note: Some entries may show the same artwork for different variants. Check the card details to verify the printing or variant, rather than relying on the image alone.

Next, check condition. If the card doesn't match the reference, work through the authenticity checks before treating it as a rare variant.
