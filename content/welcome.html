Welcome

I’m putting this guide together because I genuinely love collecting Pokémon as a hobby. Like a lot of people, I dug out my old collection one day and realized I had no idea what anything was worth anymore. The sets had changed, the market had changed, and I felt completely lost trying to figure out where to even start.

If you’re here, you might be in a similar spot: maybe you just found a childhood binder, bought a collection, or started ripping packs again and now you’re wondering what you actually have. It can be overwhelming to sort through piles of cards, strange set symbols, and a million different price websites.

My goal with this guide is to make that process a lot less confusing. Start with the question that matches where you are:

  • What card is this? Start with the name, set, number, and printing.
  • Does it look unusual? Check authenticity and possible printing errors.
  • What's it worth? Assess its condition, then compare matching sales and prices.
  • What should I do next? Protect it, track it, or decide whether grading makes sense.

By the time you work through these sections and use the Catalog Explorer to look up your cards, my hope is that you’ll feel confident looking at a card and saying, “I know what this is, I know roughly what it’s worth, and I know what to do with it next.”
