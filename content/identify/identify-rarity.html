Rarity symbols are a useful clue, but they aren't a price guide. This quick reference covers familiar English symbols; categories differ across eras and languages.

  • Black circle: Common.
  • Black diamond: Uncommon.
  • One black star: Rare.
  • Two black stars: Double Rare.
  • Two silver foil stars: Ultra Rare.
  • One gold star: Illustration Rare.
  • Two gold stars: Special Illustration Rare.
  • Three gold stars: Hyper Rare in Scarlet & Violet.

For more examples, see TCGPlayer’s rarity symbol guide and CGC’s rarity symbol guide.
