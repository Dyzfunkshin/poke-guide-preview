Tilt the card gently under a light and note where it shines. A holo has foil in the artwork and on the border of the card; a reverse holo has foil around it. Designs vary, so compare with the same card and release rather than relying on shine alone.

Also check for stamps, alternate artwork, and edition marks. In the Catalog Explorer, match the available printing and variant choices to the card in your hand before comparing prices.
