Look near the bottom edge for the collector number. In 175/203, 175 identifies the card and 203 is the size of the main numbered set. Some cards are numbered beyond that total, so a number such as 225/203 is normal.

Include letters and prefixes too. Promos and special subsets can use different numbering formats. Here, “main numbered set” means that expansion's ordinary numbering, not the original Pokémon Base Set.
