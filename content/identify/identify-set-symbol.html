The set symbol or code identifies the expansion a card belongs to. Where to look depends on the era:

  • Original English Base Set: The 1st Edition, Shadowless, and Unlimited printings have no set symbol.
  • Wizards of the Coast (WOTC): Most sets after Base Set place the symbol at the bottom-right corner of the portrait box. The e-Reader sets—Expedition, Aquapolis, and Skyridge—are exceptions, with their symbol near the bottom of the card.
  • Later sets through Sword & Shield: Look near the bottom of the card for the set symbol.
  • Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution: Look in the bottom-left corner for a printed expansion code.
  • Black Star Promos: Look for a black star marked “PROMO” and the promo number. The mark’s location varies by release.

Use the expansion symbol and code reference to match the mark on your card. This set-symbol placement reference also shows where to look across the different layouts.
